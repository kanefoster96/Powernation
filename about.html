Our story

One stop for UK cheer

For more than 20 years Power Nation has been the one stop shop for cheerleading, dance and gymnastics in the UK: bespoke practice wear and team apparel, competition uniforms and sprung floors.

Every uniform and piece of team wear is made by our in-house production team, with dedicated people checking quality at every step. That's how we offer free samples, no minimum order and lead times from five weeks.

As official distributors of Nfinity, we share their commitment to the driven athlete.
