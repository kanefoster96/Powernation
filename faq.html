FAQs

Questions coaches ask

Can't see yours? Call 01788 227 195 or email info@powernationcheer.co.uk.

Is there a minimum order?

No. Order 1 or 100, with no extra fees.

How long do uniforms take?

As little as 5 weeks once you've said yes to your sample and sizes. Lots of rhinestones can take a little longer. We'll agree your date before we start.

Is the design really free?

Yes. Our team designs your uniform and makes changes until you're happy. You don't have to order.

How does the free sample work?

Once you like the design, we make a real sample so you can try it on before ordering for the team.

Can we add athletes mid-season?

Yes. We keep your design, so new athletes can join any time and still match.

What if the uniforms don't match the sample?

We remake them for free. That's our Sample-Match Promise.

How do payments work?

Pay a deposit to start, then the rest before we send your uniforms. Big clubs can ask about monthly payments.

What's a Club Shop?

A free online shop just for your club. Parents buy your team's kit directly, we deliver to your gym, and your club earns money off future orders.

Do you make matching practice wear and bows?

Yes. Bows, practice wear, warm-ups and backpacks can all be made to match your uniform. See the shop.
