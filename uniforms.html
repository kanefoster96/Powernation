FAQs

Questions coaches ask

Can't see yours? Call 01788 227 195 or email info@powernationcheer.co.uk.

Is there a minimum order?

No. We make uniforms for a single athlete or a full program, at the same quality.

How long do uniforms take?

From 5 weeks after you approve your sample and sizes. Heavier upgrades like full rhinestone panels can add time, and we'll confirm your date before production starts.

Is the design really free?

Yes. Our in-house designers create your artwork and make changes until you're happy, with no obligation to order.

How does the free sample work?

Once you're happy with the artwork, we make a physical sample so you can check fabric, fit and finish before ordering for the team.

Can we add athletes mid-season?

Yes. We keep your design on file so new athletes can be added at any time and match the rest of the team.

What if the uniforms don't match the sample?

Our Sample-Match Promise covers you: if your finished uniforms don't match the sample you approved, we remake them free.

How do payments work?

A deposit starts production and the balance is due before dispatch. Larger programs can ask about payment plans.

What's a Club Shop?

A free private online shop for your club. Parents buy your team's practice wear, bows and warm-ups directly, we deliver to your gym, and your club earns credit on every order.

Do you make matching practice wear and bows?

Yes. Bows, practice wear, warm-ups and backpacks can all be made to match your uniform. See the shop.
