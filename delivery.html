Delivery & returns

How we send your order, and what to do if something isn't right.

Delivery

Shop orders: delivery options and prices are shown at checkout before you pay.

Team orders: sent next working day by UK Mail once ready, at £8.95 + VAT per parcel. We can deliver to your gym, your coach, or straight to each athlete's home.

Bespoke uniforms: we agree your delivery date in writing before we start, and send a named bag for each athlete.

Something not right?

Please tell us about any missing or wrong items within 10 working days of your order arriving, and we'll put it right.

Returns

Personalised and custom items are made just for you, so they can only be returned if they're faulty. For anything else, get in touch and we'll help.

Email info@powernationcheer.co.uk or call 01788 227 195.
